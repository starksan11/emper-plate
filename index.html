THE SPIRIT OF EMBER & PLATE

Rooted in simplicity.
Alive with flavour.

Our story begins with a simple idea: beautiful food should make you feel at home. EMBER & PLATE imagines a neighbourhood gathering place where contemporary dining feels personal.

In our kitchen, the seasons lead the way. We believe in the depth of an open flame, the character of a fresh ingredient, and knowing when to let good things speak for themselves.
